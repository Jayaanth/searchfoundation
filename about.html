ABOUT SEARCH FOUNDATION

Nutrition That Powers Every Victory.

A human nutrition and sports nutrition brand focused on developing products for health, performance and active lifestyles.

WHO WE ARE

Built On Nutrition.

Search Foundation is a nutrition division of N R Jagdale Group, an Indian group with a history of nutrition product development and manufacturing dating back to 1995.

Based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, Search Foundation focuses on human nutrition and sports nutrition, developing products intended to support health, physical performance and active lifestyles.

Our approach brings together product research, formulation, manufacturing and an understanding of the nutritional requirements associated with different sporting activities.

1995 N R Jagdale Group
OUR HERITAGE

Three Decades Of Nutrition Experience.

Search Foundation carries the nutrition heritage of N R Jagdale Group, with experience in developing and manufacturing nutrition products since 1995.

Over the years, the organisation has focused on developing nutrition products with attention to quality, safety and product formulation.
